# Comprehensive academy reports

## Reporting contract

Start with `[YYYY-MM-DD 학원 수업 맥락 / <class or requested scope> <student names>]`. Use the requested business date; establish the academy's date/timezone from context when resolving “today.” Do not substitute the host UTC date when the academy date differs. Do not invent a class number, attendance, or timetable.

Order the body as follows, with numbered students and numbered student subsections:

1. `<student name>`
   - `1.1 당일 흐름`: attendance, existing-homework check, lesson sequence, updates and attributable teacher decisions.
   - `1.2 <book / edition / volume>` and following book subsections: actual units in pedagogical order, with activities and occurrence-specific status beneath each unit.
   - Final student subsection: `새 숙제·지도 방향` or `오늘 할 일·확인 필요`, according to the request. Include replacements, cancellations, actual deadlines, and dependencies.
2. Repeat for each requested student in the user's order or confirmed class order.

Render plain numbered text with indented bullets, readable line breaks, and a blank line before lists. Do not replace the hierarchy with a flat task list, a score-only table, or a link-only answer. An HTML artifact may supplement the answer.

Match depth to scope. A full lesson/context report includes the requested books and unit history. A narrow question uses a concise direct answer; the full title and hierarchy below apply only to comprehensive reports. Do not dump an entire history into a one-item correction. Do not silently omit material history in a comprehensive request: group consecutive unassigned/unknown catalog ranges, and explicitly label any unavoidable continuation.

For a concrete synthetic example, read [references/report-example.md](report-example.md). Do not treat the example's dates, tasks, scores, or rules as real student facts.
